## Video embed notes

- About 67 seconds, with sound. Don't autoplay with sound; show the poster and controls. Attach the captions track (`kind="captions"`, `srclang="en"`) and link the transcript.
- For a muted hero loop, use the silent MP4, and keep the captioned version one click away.

## Wording guardrails

- Say **Windows** (maintained). macOS and Linux are experimental and unverified.
- The Studio timeline is new and still evolving; don't present planned composition features as shipped.
- Don't claim virtual-cable, Discord, Zoom or OBS support (not verified), an installer, or a store listing.
- No accessibility, security, performance or compliance guarantees.
